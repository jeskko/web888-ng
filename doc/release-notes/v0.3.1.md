Web UI additions, the channelizer's server side begun (M6), and a
skimmer fix found running on our own OS image:

* **Web UI:** a callsign or nickname per listener and a Users list
  (everyone, the frequencies of those who share them, the skimmers); a
  tuning step selector; a hover preview of the click-tune passband; the
  unit's CPU load and temperature in the status bar; synchronous AM (the
  AM button cycles AM, SAM, SAL, SAR and SAS, SAS in stereo).
* **M6, channelizer (server side, `doc/m6-channelizer.md`):** the bin
  source trait (`BinRadio`) and the iq backend's channelizer bank: 30 kHz
  bins at 60 kS/s from 7.68 MS/s (`IqConfig::bins`), with a bench
  (`binsbench`). Not yet on hardware.
* **Fix:** skimmers decoded nothing after a large clock step. On a unit
  without an RTC, NTP steps the clock by months a few seconds after
  web888d starts; the slot timing then walked to the new time one 15 s
  slot per audio block and handed out no slots. A step either way now
  restarts the slots at the next boundary.
