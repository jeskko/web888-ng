Skimmers (milestone M4, `doc/m4-skimmers.md`), and the first runs on a
Web-888:

* **Decoders in process:** FT8 (91–92 % of jt9's decodes on the test
  corpora), FT4 (89 %), WSPR-2 (a port of wsprd: all 64 of its corpus
  decodes).
  `[[skimmer]]` bands with UTC hours, on RX channels of their own, at
  low priority; they yield their channel to web users and take it back.
* **Spots:** a list beside the waterfall, call labels on the scale, each
  skimmer's range as a bar under the scale (click: tune there),
  `/api/spots`, the admin skimmer card, metrics.
* **Reporting:** PSKReporter (the stock Web-888 layout) and WSPRnet,
  from live signals only; syslog (RFC 5424/3164, UDP/TCP) and MQTT 3.1.1,
  each with its own choice of events and modes; the locator from gpsd.
* **Pseudo-stereo** in USB, LSB and CW (headphones): signals below the
  passband's middle to the left, above it to the right, as on the
  waterfall (`doc/stereo-cw.md`).
* **Waterfall:** no mirror images in the widest view (strong HF used to
  show again at 45–61 MHz) and no aliases at the edges of zoomed views;
  views can't pan or zoom out past the tunable range.
* **On a unit (2026-10-28):** the stock-driver backend runs on a
  production Web-888 with the stock bitstream and with the open one (hw
  v0.2.0): 48 k frames/s. FT8 on eight bands keeps the A9 about 84 %
  busy with no slots dropped; five FT8, two FT4 and three WSPR bands
  together are over its budget (13–15 % of FT8/FT4 slots dropped).
* Fix: decoder threads really run at `SCHED_IDLE` (musl's
  `sched_setscheduler` is a stub; the raw syscall now).
