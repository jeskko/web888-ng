Listener tools and a lighter server: Opus audio and a packed waterfall
(a listener's data down from about 370 to 126 kbit/s), HF propagation
forecasts, ADC overload events, an info page with privacy and licence
details, access limits, and less CPU per listener on the Web-888.
Details in `doc/m2-web-ui.md` §11-16.

* **Audio codecs**: Opus (the default), mu-law or PCM, chosen in the
  header. Opus takes about 2 % of an A9 core a stream and sends 24-48
  kbit/s. While a decoder panel reads the audio, the page switches to
  mu-law, because FT8 loses about 10 % of its decodes through
  narrowband Opus.
* **Packed waterfall**: each line is coded as differences between
  neighbouring bins, which takes 55 % of the bytes. A **data saver**
  sends 10 lines a second and one bin per two pixels. The status bar
  shows the codec's and the waterfall's kbit/s.
* **Listener tools**:
  * waterfall palettes, spectrum peak hold, UTC time marks down the
    waterfall and a PNG snapshot;
  * frequency memories (key m, export and import as JSON) and VFO A/B
    (key v);
  * audio recording to WAV, with silence left out on request;
  * a custom AGC speed (hang and decay) and key help (key ?).
* **IQ mode** (key i): the channel's I and Q as stereo; Rec saves an
  I/Q WAV. An **audio spectrum** panel (AF) shows the audio's spectrum
  and waterfall.
* **Automatic notch** (Notch, beside NR): removes steady carriers and
  leaves speech, CW and noise alone. On recordings, AM carriers heard
  in USB came down 27-37 dB.
* **Propagation forecasts** (Prop, `#propagation`): VOACAP, through
  hfcast (a Rust port of VOACAP, Apache-2.0), from 7 regions to the
  receiver.
  * For each region, the month's median by UTC hour: MUF, LUF, and per
    band the reliability for SSB, CW and FT8.
  * The sunspot number comes from the settings, from NOAA (fetched
    daily and cached) or from a built-in table, so forecasts work
    without a network.
  * Kp is shown, with a storm warning from Kp 5.
  * Settings: `[propagation]`, on by default; it needs `[station]
    locator`. Licences and data sources: `NOTICE-hfcast.md`.
* **ADC overload events** (the admin Status page, the log and MQTT
  under the kind `adc`, `/api/admin/ovl`): each run of overloads with
  its length and peak level, the bins that caused it, and whether the
  cause was one carrier, several, broadband noise or spread. For a
  single carrier, its frequency to a fraction of a hertz. New metrics:
  `adc_overflow_clocks_total`, `adc_peak_dbfs`.
* **Receiver settings from the admin page**: an RF attenuator
  (`[radio] atten_db`; listeners see ATT), ADC dither and PGA
  (`adc_dither`, `adc_pga`), all applied without a restart.
  `[radio] freq_offset_hz` is for a converter: the page and the
  skimmers' spots show frequencies on air.
* **Access limits** (`[limits]`):
  * `blocked` addresses: Ban in the admin Sessions page adds one;
  * `daily_minutes` per address;
  * `open_channels`: visitors without the listener password get that
    many receivers, and the page offers Log in.
* **Info page** (Info, `#info`):
  * the receiver's place, antenna, description, contact and a photo;
  * its limits;
  * a privacy section, written from the settings (`[info] privacy`,
    `contact`);
  * a reception notice (`[info] reception_notice`);
  * licences and credits: the sources, the code ported in, the data
    used and every third-party library;
  * a note on AI: the neural decoders can be wrong, and the code was
    largely written with an AI coding assistant at the author's
    direction.
* **Header**: the passband, codec and audio rate are drop-downs. Fixed:
  the waterfall's time marks flickered, and the AF spectrum looked
  overdriven on SSB.
* **CPU**: with 8 listeners and 20 skimmers, the listener and hardware
  threads went from 61 % to 50 % of both cores, which leaves the
  skimmers more time. The changes:
  * a NEON FFT (`crates/fft`): 1.2-1.9 times rustfft on the A9;
  * faster dB conversions;
  * a cheaper FT8/FT4 search: FT8 needs about 19 % fewer cycles in all;
  * one tokio worker.
* Each release now includes `web888d-vX.Y.Z.cdx.json`, a CycloneDX SBOM.

Tested:
* Unit tests. The new panels and settings were checked in headless
  Chromium on the sim backend (`ui/tools/cdp`: listener, access, codec,
  prop).
* On a Web-888 (the channelizer bitstream): each feature since
  2026-11-17 on the unit, in this order:
  * the CPU work, by A/B profiles;
  * Opus and the packed waterfall: 171 to 95 kbit/s a waterfall;
  * the PGA: a bin's level rose 3.3 dB;
  * propagation: 7 regions × 3 modes in 3.8 s, with NOAA's sunspot
    number;
  * overload events;
  * the info page.
* FT8/FT4 decodes identical on both scoring sets after the decoder and
  FFT changes, and the unit's NEON decodes match the desktop's.
* hfcast against the Fortran VOACAP: 2,941 of 3,000 random paths
  identical. Most of the rest differ where the Fortran gives different
  answers between its own builds. One input type makes hfcast panic;
  web888d catches that and shows no forecast for that region.
* Not seen on a unit yet: ADC dither's effect (it doesn't show in a
  bin's level), and a ban or daily limit on real listeners.
