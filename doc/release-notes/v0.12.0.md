Live DX cluster spots on the spectrum and in the spot list, a map of the
stations heard, OS updates from the admin page, and Prometheus metrics
brought up to date.

* **Live DX spots.** web888d follows [Spothole](https://spothole.app)'s
  stream of DX cluster spots and shows those in the receiver's range:
  * as labels on the spectrum (**Labels: DX spots**, orange), the
    latest of each station on each band; click one to tune in its mode;
  * in the **Spots** list, whose new first selector shows the skimmers'
    decodes, the DX spots or **Both**.

  On by default, DX clusters only (`[dxspots]` adds POTA, SOTA and
  other programmes, or turns it off). One connection per receiver,
  whatever the number of listeners; nothing is sent to Spothole but the
  requests.
* **A map** (the **Map** button): the stations heard, with a line from
  the receiver (a DX spot's: from its spotter).
  * Layers: the skimmers' spots, your own browser's FT8/FT4/WSPR
    decodes, and the DX spots; by band and age.
  * Great circle (centred on the receiver), globe or Mercator, with the
    greyline. Drag to turn or pan, wheel or pinch to zoom.
  * Click a station to tune to it. On a phone, tap it to see what was
    heard and tap again to tune.
  * The world map comes from the unit itself (Natural Earth, public
    domain): no outside map service.
* **OS updates from the admin page** (on the web888-ng-os card image,
  from os v0.9.0): the new **Update** tab shows the running version and
  the root slots, looks for a newer release (also by itself once a
  day), installs it into the other slot with its progress on the page
  while the receiver keeps running, and **Reboot now** starts it. Only
  the project's signed bundles install, and a system that doesn't come
  up falls back to the old one. `[update]` sets how often it looks, or
  turns it off; nothing installs without the admin.
* **Fewer false decodes reported.**
  * FT8's "RR73" sign-off is no longer taken for a locator (it put
    stations in the Arctic Ocean).
  * An uncertain FT8/FT4 decode (from the deep search, with an unknown
    `<...>` call, or at −20 dB or weaker) is uploaded to PSKReporter and
    shown on the map only once the same call is decoded again on the
    band within 15 minutes. Clear decodes go out at once, as before.
* **Metrics** (`/metrics`, `doc/metrics.md`): everything the admin pages
  show, by protocol, lease class, kind of DSP job, band and uploader,
  plus the system; a Grafana dashboard in `doc/grafana/web888d.json`.
  **Changed:** `/metrics` now answers only the addresses in
  `[web] metrics_allow` (none by default). If you scrape it, add your
  Prometheus's address or network there.
* **Smaller things:**
  * A frequency typed off the visible span moves the waterfall to it.
  * The panel buttons (sharing, Spots, Map, the decoders, Labels) moved
    to the top bar; the bottom bar keeps audio and the waterfall.
  * The status bar's numbers keep their places as they change.
  * The phone layout: the panels fit the screen, and the map is larger.

Tested: unit tests and headless browser checks of the UI (desktop and a
phone-sized touch screen); the Update tab in the emulator on the
published web888-ng-os v0.8.0 card image (it found the release on
GitHub, installed it into the other slot in about 3 minutes, rebooted
into it, and the new slot was marked good); on a Web-888 since
2026-11-12, with skimmers on 20 bands, live DX spots, the map and the
metrics scraped by Prometheus. That unit runs a system chain-loaded from
the stock firmware, without RAUC, so its Update tab says updates aren't
available there.
