The channelizer's server side (M6), run on a Web-888 with a test
bitstream, and a start-up fix for the stock front end:

* **Fix:** the front end (attenuator, RF path, ADC clock select) is
  applied when web888d starts, not only when the attenuation changes. A
  restart otherwise kept what another program had left: once the
  external clock input, with no reference on it, so the ADC ran at ~188
  MHz and every frequency was off.
* **Bins mode (`doc/m6-channelizer.md`):** with a channelizer bitstream
  (30 kHz bins at 60 kS/s from the whole HF band), receivers, skimmers
  and HPSDR take their channels from the bins (`BinRx`). The waterfall's
  wide views come from the FPGA's power line, and narrow ones (up to 17
  bins) from per-bin FFTs. HPSDR is resampled to 48 kS/s.
* **The bins tuner:** with a bitstream that also turns and decimates 32
  users' bins to 12 kS/s in the FPGA, web888d takes those users as its
  RX channels and uses the bins only for the waterfall. On the test
  unit: 20 sessions with waterfalls plus two FT8 skimmers ran with no
  audio gaps (10 with the bins on the CPU). In this mode HPSDR carries
  ±6 kHz per receiver.
* **Cheaper DSP:** the audio decimator is sized to the passband; the
  channelizer ring is parsed in place and its frames handled in runs.

The channelizer bitstreams are not released yet. With the stock
bitstream web888d works as in v0.3.1.
