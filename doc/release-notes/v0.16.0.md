The page in Finnish, Swedish and German, a Config box for the
listener's own settings, and a clearer ADC overload log. Details in
`doc/m2-web-ui.md` §18-20.

* **Languages**: the receiver page and the admin pages in Suomi,
  Svenska and Deutsch. The page takes the browser's language when it
  has a catalogue; **Config → Language** picks another or English.
  * The texts are gettext PO files in `ui/po/`, editable in any PO
    editor (Poedit, Lokalize, Weblate). `ui/po/README.md` tells how to
    fix a translation or add a language; `ui/po/GLOSSARY.md` lists the
    radio terms and how each language says them.
  * The translations were reviewed for radio terminology (the words
    radio amateurs and SWLs use) and corrected.
  * Still in English: texts the server sends (status and error
    messages), the help in the admin settings form, decoder errors, and
    of course decoded text, spots and station names.
* **Config box** (Config in the header, `#config`): the listener's
  callsign or nickname, whether others see the frequency, the data
  saver, the waterfall's colours and time marks, and the spectrum's
  labels, in one place instead of spread over the header and footer.
  On a phone the More sheet shows the same.
* **ADC overload log** (admin Status page, the log, MQTT `adc`):
  * the peak level is the highest while the overload lasts, not the
    band's level 1.5 s later;
  * a spike that leaves nothing in the band's 0.1 s power near
    clipping is called **brief** (a click or switching spike) instead
    of naming the usual strongest station as the cause;
  * a signal and its skirt in the next bin count as one signal;
  * a one-block event reads "N clocks over in one block".
* **Info** moved to the status bar beside keys and admin. The key help
  (key ?) fits its box and scrolls in short windows.

Tested:
* Unit tests, and in headless Chromium on the sim backend
  (`ui/tools/cdp`): every language for overflow in the header and
  footer (`lang.mjs`), the Config box (`config.mjs`), the key help
  (`keys.mjs`). `node scripts/i18n.mjs check` in CI: 895 of 895
  messages in each language, placeholders intact.
* On a Web-888 (the channelizer bitstream) since 2026-11-17/18: the
  Config box, the languages, and the overload log over a day of events
  (switching spikes now read "brief", peaks -0.0 dBFS).
