Fixes for running behind a reverse proxy, and limits that now work:

* **Reverse proxies**: list them in `[web] trusted_proxies` (addresses or
  networks, applied without a restart). For requests from them, the
  client's address comes from `X-Forwarded-For` (the rightmost hop that
  isn't a trusted proxy, so clients can't make one up) and
  `X-Forwarded-Proto: https` keeps login cookies `Secure`. Before, every
  user behind a proxy had the proxy's address: `per_ip` (default 4)
  limited them all together and failed logins locked everyone out.
* **Session and idle limits**: `[limits] session_minutes` and
  `idle_minutes` were accepted but did nothing. Web sessions now get a
  notice a minute before, then end (close codes 4001 / 4002); any input
  resets the idle time, admins are exempt. The page shows the reason
  with a Reconnect button instead of reconnecting by itself.
